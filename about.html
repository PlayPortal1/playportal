Why I built PlayPortal

Recruiting agencies charge thousands of dollars for something that, honestly, doesn't need to cost that much. But my problem with agencies isn't only the price — it's the approach. I don't believe you need someone to do the whole process for you.

When I was being recruited, being directly involved and in contact with coaches and university staff made the process personal. Those people guided me through everything I needed — and when I arrived on campus, I already knew them. That head start matters more than most athletes realize.

Here's the truth about recruiting: it's mostly marketing. It's about getting yourself in front of as many coaches as possible, and there's no better way to do that than reaching out to all of them — then filtering through the responses to find the university that's genuinely right for you. That's exactly what PlayPortal is built to do: maximum visibility, at a price that makes sense, with you in control of the conversations.

Who handles your campaign

Me. Not a call center, not an algorithm, not an "account team." I personally review every profile, write every recruiting email, and manage every campaign that goes out. When you have a question, the person who answers is the same person who played this sport at the level you're aiming for and sat exactly where you're sitting.

For a big agency you're one file in a stack. Here, I know your name, your game, and your goals — because there's no one between us.
